Is the estimate really free?

Yes. We visit your home, look at the floors and the subfloor, talk through what you want, and then send you the estimate. No cost and no obligation.

What types of floors do you install?

Hardwood, custom wood patterns like herringbone, luxury vinyl plank (LVP) and tile. Other floor types are reviewed project by project. We do not install carpet or sheet vinyl.

Can my old hardwood be refinished instead of replaced?

Often, yes. Sanding removes scratches, stains and worn finish so the wood can be stained and sealed again. We check the thickness and condition of your boards during the visit and tell you honestly which option makes sense.

Can I buy the materials myself?

Yes. We work with materials you supply or we source them for you, depending on the project.

Do you only do floors?

Floors are our focus, but we also handle trim and finish carpentry, tile and ceramic, and interior painting, so one crew can finish the whole room.
