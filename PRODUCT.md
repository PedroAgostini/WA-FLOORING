## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML, CSS and vanilla JS. Three.js loaded from a pinned CDN build for the 3D section. Hosted on the client's existing Hostinger account (access pending). No build step.

## Users

American homeowners in the Moncks Corner and greater Charleston, South Carolina area, many of them families and higher income households, though service is not restricted to them. They arrive mostly from Google Ads and Google search on a phone, comparing local flooring contractors before asking for an estimate. They prefer to text (SMS); the owner prefers phone calls and on-site visits.

## Product Purpose

One page institutional and lead generation site for WA Flooring Specialist. Success means a visitor requests a free on-site estimate by form, call or text. Supports paid traffic (US$ 25 to 30/day) focused on floor installation and hardwood sanding and refinishing, and reduces dependence on referrals.

## Positioning

Owner led crew working in flooring since 2010 (company founded 2018). Custom wood work, including designs and patterns in wood, executed by the same hands that quote it. Direct contact with the owner and an on-site visit before any estimate.

## Operating Context

First contact is usually a text message, often from a referral. The owner visits the property, assesses the job, then sends the estimate. Works with client supplied or company supplied materials. Occasionally serves Georgia and North Carolina, but campaigns stay near Moncks Corner.

## Product Principles

1. Every screen leads to an estimate request by form, call or text.
2. Show real craft, never promise work the crew does not do.
3. Owner led and local is the core trust argument.
4. Fast on a phone on a cellular connection, since ads drive most traffic.
