# Image provenance

## Current site images

The site uses 15 original AI-generated illustrative interiors in `generated/`, created with the built-in `image_gen` tool on 2026-11-09 at the user's request. These are fictional scenes inspired by residential architecture in the Moncks Corner / Charleston, South Carolina region. They are not photographs of actual properties or completed WA Flooring Specialist projects.

Original PNG files, exact prompts, source paths, dimensions and the asset manifest are saved in `../output/imagegen/`. WebP files have full-size, 960px and 480px variants; the hero also has a JPEG for social sharing. Encoding and resizing do not retouch image content.

Regional visual research (context only; no listing photographs were copied into the generated assets):
- https://www.carolinaone.com/p/1662-Locals-Street-Summerville-SC-29485/dmgid_182594145
- https://www.redfin.com/SC/Mount-Pleasant/517-Whilden-St-29464/home/108184073
